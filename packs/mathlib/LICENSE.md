# Licence of the mathlib pack

`rose_mathlib/catalog.jsonl` contains theorem names, statements and docstrings extracted from
[Lean mathlib4](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), Copyright the mathlib
contributors, licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The commit is recorded in every card's `url`.
Proofs are not included; each statement was checked by Lean at that commit.

Changes: statements are cut at `:=`, whitespace is normalised, a search text (`intent`) is added.
